# Qwen3 Embedding 8B

## Overview
Qwen3-Embedding-8B is the largest of the Qwen3 embedding models released on 2025-06-05 (Hugging Face repo created 2025-06-03; technical report arXiv 2506.05176). OpenRouter serves it on its embeddings endpoint as `qwen/qwen3-embedding-8b` and lists a 40,960-token context, the config.json position limit; Qwen documents a 32K sequence length. It fine-tunes Qwen3-8B-Base (36 layers, 7.6B parameters) through contrastive pre-training on weakly supervised pairs generated by Qwen3, supervised training and model merging, and takes the final [EOS] token's hidden state as the embedding. Output is 4,096 dimensions, and any size from 32 to 4,096 can be requested. Queries take a one-sentence instruction (`Instruct: {task}\nQuery:{query}`); documents take none. Qwen publishes GGUF files, and Ollama's untagged `qwen3-embedding` (`latest`) is this model.

## Strengths
- First on the MTEB multilingual leaderboard on 2025-06-05 with 70.58 Mean (Task); Qwen also reports 75.22 on MTEB (English, v2) and 73.84 on C-MTEB. On 2026-11-01 the leaderboard still showed 70.58 (fourth) and 75.22.
- Best of its family in Qwen's multilingual table on retrieval (70.88), STS (81.08) and bitext mining (80.89).
- 100+ natural and programming languages.
- Apache 2.0; Sentence Transformers, Transformers, vLLM and Text Embeddings Inference usage is documented.

## Limitations
- The 4B model comes within about a point (69.45) at roughly half the size.
- Omitting the query instruction costs about 1% to 5% in retrieval, per Qwen; instructions should be in English.
- Full 4,096-dimensional vectors are large to store unless truncated.
- Pooling takes the last token, so batched inference needs left padding or the card's `last_token_pool` code.

## Good for
- High-quality open multilingual and code retrieval on a GPU.
- Two-stage search paired with Qwen3-Reranker.
- A strong baseline when evaluating new embedders.
