# Qwen3 235B A22B Instruct 2507

## Overview
Qwen3-235B-A22B-Instruct-2507 is the updated non-thinking version of Qwen3-235B-A22B, released on 2025-07-21 according to the Qwen GitHub README. The Hugging Face card gives 235B total and 22B activated parameters (234B non-embedding), 94 layers, grouped-query attention with 64 query and 4 key-value heads, 128 experts with 8 active, and a context of 262,144 tokens natively, extendable to 1,010,000 tokens. It runs only in non-thinking mode and never emits think blocks. OpenRouter lists a 262,144-token context, 16,384 output tokens, a June 2025 knowledge cutoff, and tool calling with structured outputs. On Ollama the tags qwen3:235b-instruct and qwen3:235b-a22b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M are this model. The Qwen3.5 to Qwen3.8 generations, which have their own cards, supersede it.

## Strengths
- Qwen reports MMLU-Pro 83.0, GPQA 77.5, SuperGPQA 62.6, SimpleQA 54.3, AIME25 70.3, HMMT25 55.4, ARC-AGI 41.8, ZebraLogic 95.0 and LiveCodeBench v6 51.8, ahead of Kimi K2 and DeepSeek-V3-0324 on most of these in its table.
- Qwen reports IFEval 88.7, Arena-Hard v2 79.2, BFCL-v3 70.9, TAU2-Retail 74.6, MultiIF 77.5 and PolyMATH 50.2.
- Artificial Analysis (Qwen3 235B A22B 2507 Instruct): GPQA Diamond 75.3%, IFBench 46.1%, Humanity's Last Exam 11.1%.
- Low OpenRouter price at 0.09 USD input and 0.55 USD output per million tokens.

## Limitations
- Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 12, Omniscience -44, Terminal-Bench Hard 15.2%, tau2-bench 33.3%, long-context reasoning 33.9%.
- 43.6 output tokens per second and 1.29 s to first token in Artificial Analysis' measurements.
- No thinking mode, text input only, and 16,384 output tokens on OpenRouter.
- 235B total parameters need a multi-GPU node to self-host.

## Good for
- General assistants that need wide knowledge and multilingual coverage without visible reasoning.
- Long-context document work up to 256K tokens.
- Tool-calling agents on a budget.
